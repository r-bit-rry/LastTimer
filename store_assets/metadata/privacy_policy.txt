This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

## Data Collection and Usage

### No Personal Data Collection

LastTimer is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or data from our users. The app functions entirely on your device without requiring any account creation or online connectivity.

### App Usage Data

All data created within the app (timers, settings, templates, etc.) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this information.

### Permissions

LastTimer requires the following permissions:
- **Notification Access**: To show timer notifications
- **Start at Boot**: To restart active timers if your device reboots
- **Run in Background**: To allow timers to function even when the app is not in the foreground
- **Internet Access**: Only used for crash reporting (which does not include personal information)

## Data Security

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, the security of your information depends on your device's security measures. We recommend keeping your device updated and secured with a passcode.

## Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13.
