# LastTimer - Minimalist Timer App

## Short Description
An elegant, minimalist timer app with multiple timer types, groups, and templates to organize your time effectively.

## Full Description
LastTimer is a comprehensive time management app designed with a clean, minimalist aesthetic that brings together powerful timing capabilities in a simple, intuitive interface.

🕒 MULTIPLE TIMER TYPES
• Countdown Timers: Set precise timers for specific durations
• Stopwatch: Track elapsed time with lap functionality
• Date Countdown: Count down to specific dates and events

⚡ ADVANCED FEATURES
• Cascading Timers: Chain multiple timers to run sequentially - perfect for interval training, cooking complex recipes, or any multistep process
• Repeating Timers: Set timers that automatically restart - ideal for regular reminders or interval workouts
• Timer Templates: Save your frequently used timer configurations as templates for quick access
• Custom Names & Categories: Organize your timers by name and category for easy reference

🎨 BEAUTIFUL USER EXPERIENCE
• Intuitive, Minimalist Design: Clean interface that's easy to navigate
• Dark/Light Theme Support: Choose the theme that suits your preference
• Tab-based Navigation: Easily switch between different timer types
• Lock Screen Controls: Control your timers without unlocking your device
• Home Screen Widgets: Start, pause, and stop timers directly from your home screen

⚙️ SMART FUNCTIONALITY
• Background Operation: Timers continue to run even when the app is closed
• Notifications: Get alerts when your timers finish
• Battery Efficient: Designed to minimize battery usage while maintaining accuracy

LastTimer is the perfect companion for:
• Fitness and workouts (interval training, HIIT sessions)
• Cooking and baking (keep track of multiple dishes simultaneously)
• Studying (Pomodoro technique)
• Productivity (time blocking)
• Meditation and mindfulness sessions
• And any other activity where time management matters
